# CAIOS v5.2 — Image rights gate

## Safety model

Every WordPress draft must reference one active, reviewer-approved image-rights record. The record preserves the direct image URL, source page, creator, license or permission, required credit, alt text, retrieval time, and commercial-use decision.

The approval RPC is limited to authenticated administrators, editors, and reviewers. Story approval remains blocked until the image is approved and the rights checklist is complete. WordPress delivery remains draft-only and never schedules, publishes, or publicizes the post.

## Eligible image sources

- Openverse results explicitly marked CC0
- WordPress Photo Directory assets after human license review
- Official press assets with recorded permission evidence
- Chilemaniacs-owned images
- The approved Chilemaniacs branded fallback

An image URL alone is not rights evidence. The reviewer must inspect the source record and confirm commercial website use before approval.

## Editorial sequence

1. Open the story workspace and search for up to three CC0 candidates, or open the manual rights form.
2. Inspect the source page and license or permission evidence.
3. Enter accurate, useful alt text and the required credit line.
4. Confirm commercial use and, when applicable, cropping or modification rights.
5. Approve the image-rights record.
6. Complete the editorial checklist and record human story approval.
7. Prepare and manually dispatch the WordPress draft.
8. Confirm WordPress received a draft with the selected featured image and attribution metadata.

## Release validation

- Migration `20260729190000_v5_2_image_rights_gate.sql` is present in the target Supabase migration ledger.
- `story_image_rights` has row-level security enabled.
- Anonymous roles cannot read rights records or execute the approval RPC.
- The authenticated newsroom role can read rights records and eligible reviewer roles can execute the RPC.
- Lint, type checking, tests, and the production build pass.
- A staging story cannot be approved without an active rights record.
- A staging WordPress draft receives the approved featured image and remains `draft`.

## Explicit exclusions

This milestone does not perform automatic image selection, infer copyright permission, approve images without a human reviewer, publish or schedule WordPress posts, publicize posts, or retry failed media uploads automatically.
